# MacTech CUI Vault — IR Tabletop & AAR Evidence Kit

## C3PAO Walkthrough

**Audience:** CMMC Third-Party Assessor Organization (C3PAO) staff conducting a CMMC 2.0 Level 2 certification assessment of a MacTech CUI Vault customer.

**Scope:** Assessment of NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 controls **IR.L2-3.6.1**, **IR.L2-3.6.2**, **IR.L2-3.6.3** as implemented through the MacTech CUI Vault Incident Response Exercise & After-Action Report Evidence Package.

**Document version:** v1.0 — produced alongside Phase 7 hardening of the IR Tabletop product line.

---

## 1. What this product is — and what it is not

The MacTech CUI Vault IR Tabletop & AAR Evidence Kit operationalizes the **technical** portion of incident response for the customer's CUI Vault. It produces a customer-specific tabletop exercise plan, runs the customer through it, captures their decisions, and emits an assessment-defensible evidence bundle.

It does **not** replace:

- The customer's organizational incident response plan
- The customer's named IR team
- The customer's authority to make external reporting determinations
- The customer's responsibility to remediate findings

The Shared Responsibility Matrix (one of the 10 artifacts in every package) makes this split explicit per row.

## 3. IR.L2-3.6.1 — Establish operational incident-handling capability

> "Establish an operational incident-handling capability for organizational systems that includes preparation, detection, analysis, containment, recovery, and user response activities."

### NIST SP 800-171A Assessment Objectives

| ID | Objective | Where the evidence lives |
|---|---|---|
| **3.6.1[a]** | Operational incident-handling capability is established | `IR-Tabletop-Exercise-Plan.docx` (sections 1, 5, 6) + Customer's IR Plan (customer-provided evidence) |
| **3.6.1[b]** | The capability includes preparation | Scenario library (`ir_scenarios.injects_json`), Facilitator Guide, ROE in `IR-Facilitator-Guide.docx` |
| **3.6.1[c]** | …includes detection and analysis | Inject T+0 / T+15 in every scenario test detection; T+30 / T+45 test analysis (scoping, evidence preservation). Captured in `ir_inject_responses` |
| **3.6.1[d]** | …includes containment | Inject T+30 in SCEN-A (account disable), T+15 in SCEN-B (session termination), T+0 in SCEN-C (endpoint isolation) |
| **3.6.1[e]** | …includes recovery | Inject T+75 in every scenario tests recovery acceptance with evidence preservation continuation |
| **3.6.1[f]** | …includes user response activities | Inject T+0 in SCEN-A (user reports inability to access vault); user notification path threaded through SCEN-A T+45 and SCEN-B T+30 |

### How to assess

1. Open `/assessor/ir-tabletop` in control-plane. Confirm at least one exercise exists for the customer with `status = approved`.
2. Click into the exercise. The detail page renders:
    - **Scope statement** — verify it explicitly bounds the test to the CUI Vault assessment boundary, no broader.
    - **Methodology + justification** — verify the customer chose tabletop / walkthrough / functional with a written rationale.
    - **Inject timeline** — confirm a status (pass / partial / fail / not_reached) is recorded against every inject. Read the actual response notes for the controls you care about.
    - **Findings + corrective actions** — verify gaps were turned into trackable POA&M-aligned rows.
3. Cross-check the customer's IR Plan (a customer-provided artifact, not generated by MacTech) against the scenario being tested. The Plan is what 3.6.1[a] requires; the scenario is the test of it.

### Defensible evidence summary for 3.6.1

- ✅ Operational capability **tested** (not just documented) — one or more `ir_exercises` with status `approved` and a populated AAR
- ✅ Each of the 6 sub-objectives covered by at least one scenario inject with an objective pass criterion
- ✅ Pass/Partial/Fail evaluation is **objective** — pass criteria are written into `ir_scenarios.injects_json` at seed time and snapshotted onto the exercise at creation, not negotiated post-hoc

## 4. IR.L2-3.6.2 — Track, document, and report incidents

> "Track, document, and report incidents to designated officials and/or authorities both internal and external to the organization."

### NIST SP 800-171A Assessment Objectives

| ID | Objective | Where the evidence lives |
|---|---|---|
| **3.6.2[a]** | Incidents are tracked | `ir_exercises` row per simulated incident; `audit_logs` filtered to `resourceType IN ('ir_exercise', 'ir_aar', 'ir_exercise_bundle', …)` |
| **3.6.2[b]** | Incidents are documented | `IR-AAR-Report.docx` per exercise; archived in `ir_exercise_bundles.manifest_json` + `archived_state_snapshot_json` |
| **3.6.2[c]** | Authorities to whom incidents are reported are identified | `ir_exercises.reporting_authorities_json` captures DIBNet/DC3, contract officer name+email, MSSP, additional channels |
| **3.6.2[d]** | Designated officials within the organization are notified | Inject T+45 in SCEN-A tests customer-internal notification; participant table records who was notified |
| **3.6.2[e]** | Incidents are reported to appropriate authorities | Inject T+60 in SCEN-A tests the **DFARS 252.204-7012(c) 72-hour reporting decision**; SCEN-B T+60 tests the contract-officer notification path |

### How to assess

1. On the exercise detail view, locate the "Reporting authorities" subsection of the AAR. Confirm:
    - DIBNet/DC3 enabled flag is correct for the contract under test
    - Contract officer name + email are present (and not stale — ask the customer when they last verified)
    - MSSP entry present if the customer uses one
2. Confirm the inject timeline shows a recorded decision for the **72-hour clock** at T+60 — both the anchor time the customer chose AND the documented reporting-or-not determination
3. Cross-check the customer's contractual reporting obligations. DFARS 7012 + any contract-specific clauses.

### Defensible evidence summary for 3.6.2

- ✅ Tracking infrastructure exists at the DB level (`ir_exercises`, `audit_logs`)
- ✅ Reporting paths captured **per exercise** rather than once per organization (so contract changes don't invalidate prior tests)
- ✅ The 72-hour DFARS clock is exercised explicitly with an objective pass criterion

## 5. IR.L2-3.6.3 — Test the organizational incident response capability

> "Test the organizational incident response capability."

### NIST SP 800-171A Assessment Objectives

| ID | Objective | Where the evidence lives |
|---|---|---|
| **3.6.3[a]** | The organizational incident response capability is tested | `ir_exercises.status = approved` for at least one row, with executed_at populated |
| **3.6.3[b]** | The test is documented | `ir_exercise_bundles` row per archived bundle, with `manifest_json` + `archived_state_snapshot_json` |

### How to assess

1. On the assessor list page (`/assessor/ir-tabletop`), confirm the customer has at least one **approved** exercise within the assessment cycle window (default: last 12 months).
2. Open the exercise detail. Confirm:
    - **AAR drafter ≠ AAR approver** — the page shows a red C3PAO ALERT banner if not. The DB CHECK constraint `ir_aars_drafter_approver_distinct` enforces this at the database level too.
    - **Approval signature ref** is present on the AAR row (format: `clerk-attest:{email}:{unix-millis}`) — gives both identity and timestamp of approval.
    - **Archived bundle** exists (one or more entries in the Bundle table) with a non-truncated `manifestSha256` (64 hex chars).
    - **Frozen state snapshot** is present on `ir_exercise_bundles.archived_state_snapshot_json` — this is the authoritative read of "what was tested" even if subsequent edits change live data.
3. Verify the **annual cadence**. The customer should be re-running this every ~12 months. The training-app dashboard surfaces a banner when any exercise is 10+ months past its `executed_at` — confirm the customer is acting on it.

### Defensible evidence summary for 3.6.3

- ✅ Test happened (`ir_exercises.status = approved` is a meaningful state machine outcome, not a label that can be flipped without going through the system)
- ✅ Test is documented in the AAR (`ir_aars` row + `IR-AAR-Report.docx` artifact + frozen state snapshot)
- ✅ Drafter ≠ approver enforced at three layers: API auth check, app pre-check, database CHECK constraint

## 6. Cross-cutting evidence

### 6.1 Chain of custody

The bundle's hash chain works as follows:

```
exercise + participants + AAR + findings + CARs + inject responses
        ↓ (at archive time, frozen into archived_state_snapshot_json)
              ↓ (rendered through generators)
                    ↓
              10 files (DOCX/XLSX/PDF/JSON)
                    ↓ each gets SHA-256
              Manifest JSON (lists files + per-file SHA-256)
                    ↓ SHA-256
              ir_exercise_bundles.manifest_sha256
                    ↓ X-IR-Manifest-Sha256 header on download
              Customer's downloaded ZIP
```

**To verify a customer-provided ZIP matches what control-plane has on file:**
```bash
sha256sum -- <bundle.zip>/Evidence-Manifest.json
# Compare to:
SELECT manifest_sha256 FROM ir_exercise_bundles WHERE id = '<bundle uuid>';
```

If the values match, the bundle is the same one that was archived. (Note: this verifies the manifest, not every file inside the ZIP. To verify every file, extract the ZIP and recompute SHA-256 for each file against `manifest.files[].sha256`.)

### 6.2 Audit trail

Every state-changing operation writes an `audit_logs` row with `resourceType` in:

- `ir_exercise` (create, participants_added, inject_responses_recorded)
- `ir_aar` (aar_drafted, aar_approved)
- `ir_exercise_bundle` (bundle_archived)
- `ir_finding` (finding_updated)
- `ir_corrective_action` (corrective_action_updated)

The assessor detail page renders the last 50 events at the bottom. Full history available via:
```sql
SELECT * FROM audit_logs
WHERE resource_type LIKE 'ir_%'
   OR (details::jsonb)->>'exerciseId' = '<exercise uuid>'
ORDER BY created_at;
```

### 6.3 Records retention

Every package carries a retention statement anchored to:

1. Two consecutive CMMC 2.0 Level 2 assessment cycles (3 years × 2)
2. FAR 4.703 contract records retention norms
3. The customer's internal records schedule

Default is **6 years from exercise execution date**, configurable per organization via `organizations.default_ir_retention_years`. Per-exercise overrides via `ir_exercises.retention_until`. Legal hold supersedes the retention floor (`ir_exercises.legal_hold_active`).

**No artifact is auto-deleted by the platform.** Post-retention disposal requires explicit multi-party admin authorization and is logged.

### 6.4 Methodology defensibility

The customer must justify their methodology choice (tabletop / walkthrough / functional) in writing at exercise creation. The justification is stored in `ir_exercises.methodology_justification` (NOT NULL, ≥20 chars enforced at the API). The justification appears verbatim in the Plan DOCX and the AAR DOCX.

A C3PAO challenge of "why tabletop?" is answered by reading this field.

## 7. Known limitations (Phase 8 backlog)

### 7.1 Bundle byte archival

Currently, control-plane stores **the manifest** (with per-file SHA-256s) and the **frozen state snapshot** but not the actual bundle bytes. Bytes are regeneratable from the snapshot using the same generator code.

**Implication for assessors:** verifying a customer's downloaded ZIP requires control-plane to be available so the manifest hash can be compared. The bytes themselves are not in archival cold storage today.

**Mitigation:** The frozen state snapshot (`archived_state_snapshot_json`) is the authoritative record. Even without the bytes, an assessor can read the snapshot and see exactly what was tested, by whom, and what was decided.

**Phase 8 plan:** Wire `@azure/storage-blob` (already a control-plane dependency) so the bundle ZIP is stored in Azure Government Blob Storage at archive time. Same hash chain, but bytes are durable.

### 7.2 Generator non-determinism

The DOCX, XLSX, and PDF generators embed timestamps and (for PDF) random object IDs at generation time. Re-running the generators with the same inputs does not produce byte-identical output today.

**Implication for assessors:** the manifest's per-file SHA-256s reflect the bytes at archive time. Re-generating the bundle later will produce different byte content with different per-file hashes.

**Mitigation:** the **archived state snapshot** is what the assessor reads. The generated documents are presentations of that snapshot, not the source of truth.

**Phase 8 plan:** Thread `archivedAt` from the snapshot into all three generators so re-runs produce byte-identical output (where library APIs allow).

### 7.3 Live control titles

The Control Mapping Matrix XLSX uses a hard-coded subset of NIST 800-171 control titles for the IR/AC/AU/SI/SC/CM controls referenced by the seed scenarios. Other controls show "—" for title.

**Phase 8 plan:** Fetch from control-plane's `data/control-library.v1.json` via a new bridge endpoint `GET /api/ir-tabletop/control-library`.

### 7.4 Live MacTech-provided evidence

The CUI Vault Technical Evidence Summary PDF lists 13 boilerplate items describing what MacTech provides per tenant. These are static — they don't pull live values from `governance_evidence_files` or `evidence_runs` for the specific customer's vault.

**Phase 8 plan:** Pull tenant-specific evidence inventory at generation time, replacing the boilerplate with the live signal.

### 7.5 Integration tests

Today only the generator smoke test (`npm run smoke:ir-tabletop`) runs in CI. The bridge auth, archive flow, and assessor view paths are not covered by automated tests.

**Phase 8 plan:** Add a vitest suite that hits a local control-plane instance with the bridge HMAC.

## 8. Appendix — Assessor CLI commands

For an assessor with read access to the control-plane Postgres:

```sql
-- All approved exercises for an org in the last 12 months
SELECT id, name, methodology, executed_at, retention_until
FROM ir_exercises
WHERE organization_id = '<org uuid>'
  AND status = 'approved'
  AND executed_at > now() - interval '12 months'
ORDER BY executed_at DESC;

-- Verify drafter != approver for every approved AAR
SELECT
  e.name,
  a.drafted_by_user_id,
  a.approved_by_user_id,
  a.drafted_by_user_id = a.approved_by_user_id AS conflict
FROM ir_aars a
JOIN ir_exercises e ON e.id = a.exercise_id
WHERE e.organization_id = '<org uuid>'
  AND a.approved_at IS NOT NULL;
-- Should return zero rows with conflict = true (DB CHECK enforces this)

-- Audit timeline for a specific exercise
SELECT created_at, action, user_id, details
FROM audit_logs
WHERE resource_type LIKE 'ir_%'
   OR (details::jsonb)->>'exerciseId' = '<exercise uuid>'
ORDER BY created_at;

-- Hash a downloaded bundle's manifest and compare to control-plane's record
SELECT manifest_sha256, manifest_json, archived_state_snapshot_json
FROM ir_exercise_bundles
WHERE id = '<bundle uuid>';
```
