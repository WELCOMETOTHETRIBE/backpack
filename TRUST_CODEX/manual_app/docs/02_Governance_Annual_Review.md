# Governance Annual Review & Sign-off

Use this module for **Governance / Policy (Class B)** controls (and any control with a governance component).

## Goal

Show an assessor that governance is **real**, **current**, and **operationalized**:

- policies/SOPs exist
- ownership is defined
- records prove execution
- the program is reviewed at least annually (or as required)

## Annual review checklist (typical)

- Confirm each referenced policy/SOP is current and approved.
- Confirm required records exist (training rosters, reviews, change approvals, IR exercises, risk reviews, etc.).
- Record the annual review meeting/attestation.
- Update evidence index pointers if artifact locations changed.
- Set the next review date.

## What to capture as evidence

- Approved policy/SOP revision + approval record
- Meeting minutes or attestation form
- Evidence of execution (records)
- A short summary memo of what changed (or “no changes”)
