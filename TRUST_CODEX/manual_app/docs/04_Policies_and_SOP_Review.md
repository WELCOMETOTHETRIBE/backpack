# Policies & SOP Review Checklist

Use this module to ensure governance artifacts are assessment-ready.

## Open the embedded governance bundle

- Governance bundle index (templates): `../governance/GOVERNANCE_BUNDLE_INDEX.md`

## Checklist

- Policy exists for each governed control family.
- SOP exists where procedures are required.
- Each document includes:
  - purpose/scope
  - roles/responsibilities
  - step-by-step procedure (where applicable)
  - recordkeeping expectations
  - review cadence
- Each document has an approval record.
- Records exist to prove the SOP was followed.
- Annual review is recorded.

## Common record artifacts

- Access approvals
- Training rosters
- Risk review minutes
- POA&M updates
- Change control tickets
- Incident response tabletop/exercise records
