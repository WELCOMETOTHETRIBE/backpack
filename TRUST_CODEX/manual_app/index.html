Governance

Governance closeout is document-specific: review the required Policies/SOPs, sign each one on cadence, and produce an evidence artifact under C:\\evidence.
Document review & sign-off (Policies / SOPs)
Review one document at a time, then sign + date. The manual advances to the next document until completion. When required documents are signed, Governance controls can be adjudicated with an auditor-defensible record.
